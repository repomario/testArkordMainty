# Performance Volumes and Targets

## Problem

Realistic and maximum data volumes, quantitative response-time targets, and whether formal service-level agreements are required have not been established.

## Source of Uncertainty

The proposal requires scalable access to potentially large operational lists but supplies no workload measurements, thresholds, or service commitments.

## Project Impact

The uncertainty limits objective agreement on performance expectations and acceptance of operational responsiveness at anticipated usage levels.

## Technical Impact

Capacity assumptions, measurable performance criteria, and the level of performance validation cannot be fixed until volumes, response targets, and any SLA obligations are known.
