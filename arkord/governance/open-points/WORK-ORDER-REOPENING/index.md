# Completed Work Order Reopening Policy

## Problem

The policy for reopening a completed Work Order is not established, including whether reopening is permitted, which roles may perform it, and which lifecycle transitions are valid.

## Source of Uncertainty

The approved scope establishes completion and workflow integrity but does not define an exceptional post-completion transition policy or the authority to invoke one.

## Project Impact

The unresolved policy affects how users correct or continue completed interventions and how completion is understood in operational history.

## Technical Impact

The decision affects backend transition validation, role authorization, preservation of lifecycle history, and which reopening events fall within operational auditability.
