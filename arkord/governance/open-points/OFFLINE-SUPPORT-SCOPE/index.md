# Offline Support Scope

## Problem

Whether and to what extent brief-connectivity-interruption support is required in V1 is unresolved.

## Source of Uncertainty

The approved project understanding identifies this decision as unresolved and provides no Human Owner resolution.

## Project Impact

The field experience during interrupted connectivity has no established V1 commitment.

## Technical Impact

Client synchronization, caching, and offline state behavior must not be assumed.
