# Attachment Limits and Retention

## Problem

Maximum file size, maximum attachment count, supported formats, and retention policy for photographic attachments are not established.

## Source of Uncertainty

Photographs are included in operational scope, but the approved proposal provides no quantitative limits, format set, or retention duration.

## Project Impact

The unresolved rules affect what users can attach, whether older evidence remains available, and how attachment-related expectations are communicated during maintenance work.

## Technical Impact

The decision affects storage consumption, upload validation, untrusted-content handling, retrieval behavior, and lifecycle management of stored files.
