# Mainty V1 Capability Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Access and Operational Foundations

Establish authentication, role-based authorization, user administration, and the Facility and Asset master data required by maintenance operations.

## Milestone 2 — Maintenance Intake and Work Control

Establish Maintenance Request reporting and Work Order creation and management, including their applicable Facility, Asset, and request relationships.

## Milestone 3 — Assignment, Execution, and Collaboration

Enable Technician assignment, basic scheduling, operational views of assigned and time-sensitive work, field execution, and collaboration through notes, comments, and photographs.

## Milestone 4 — Operational Visibility

Provide activity and maintenance history, Work Order search and filtering, operational dashboard indicators, and notifications within the application.

## Milestone 5 — Responsive V1 Hardening

Harden the established V1 capabilities for reliable, secure, responsive use across desktop, tablet, and smartphone, with particular attention to simple and efficient Technician workflows on mobile devices.

## Possible Future Evolution

Multi-organization support, suppliers, spare parts or inventory, preventive maintenance, external notifications, offline support, QR codes, integrations, and advanced analytics remain non-binding future possibilities. They are not committed V1 milestones.
