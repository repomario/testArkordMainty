# Mainty Governance Roadmap

## V1 — consolidate the operational core

V1 establishes a coherent, responsive maintenance-management capability for a single organization:

- authentication, active/inactive users, roles, and role-consistent access;
- Facility management and Asset management within Facilities;
- Maintenance Requests and Work Orders across reporting, planning, assignment, scheduling, execution, and history;
- Technician-oriented assigned-work, daily, priority, overdue, and field-execution experiences;
- comments, photographs, and traceable maintenance history;
- Work Order text search and combinable filtering;
- an operational dashboard for open, in-progress, completed, Critical, and overdue Work Orders;
- internal operational indications for assignments and important or Critical conditions; and
- a responsive web interface for desktop, tablet, and smartphone.

This is a Governance evolution statement, not an implementation sequence or authorization to begin Delivery. Unsettled V1 boundaries remain represented by Open Points.

## Possible future evolution

The architecture should permit later consideration of multi-organization support. Other non-committed possibilities are suppliers/providers, spare-parts management, recurring preventive maintenance, external notifications, offline capabilities, QR codes, integrations, and advanced analytics. Their presence here does not place them in V1 or resolve related Open Points.
