# Facility Management

## Capability

Authorized users can maintain the physical Facilities within the managed organization.

## Functional Scope

Facility management includes creation, modification, consultation, and deactivation.

## Boundaries and Rules

A deactivated Facility remains available to preserve and interpret historical maintenance records. Deactivation distinguishes an unmanaged Facility from deletion of its history.
