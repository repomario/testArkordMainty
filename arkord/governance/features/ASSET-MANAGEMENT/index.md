# Asset Management

## Capability

Mainty maintains Assets located at managed Facilities and makes their maintenance context available to operations.

## Functional Scope

Asset records include identification, Facility association, location, state, and consultation of maintenance history.

## Boundaries and Rules

Each Asset is associated with a Facility. Changes to current Asset information must not make historical maintenance activity uninterpretable.
