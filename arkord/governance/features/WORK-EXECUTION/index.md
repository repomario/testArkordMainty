# Maintenance Work Execution

## Capability

Technicians can carry out assigned interventions through a simple operational workflow suitable for field use.

## Functional Scope

Execution supports starting, updating, and completing work; viewing relevant Asset information; and recording performed work with notes and photographs.

## Boundaries and Rules

Recorded status and completion information must correspond to the intervention's actual lifecycle. Completion records the performed work and does not erase earlier operational information.
