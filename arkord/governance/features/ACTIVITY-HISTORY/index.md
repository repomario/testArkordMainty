# Maintenance Activity History

## Capability

Mainty retains an intelligible history of maintenance interventions and significant Work Order operations.

## Functional Scope

Users can consult intervention history for relevant operational subjects, including Assets, and trace significant changes to Work Orders.

## Boundaries and Rules

Historical information must remain interpretable when related users, Facilities, or Assets later become inactive. Current-record changes must not rewrite the record of past activity.
