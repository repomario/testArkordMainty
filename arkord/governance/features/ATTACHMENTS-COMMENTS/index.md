# Attachments and Operational Comments

## Capability

Users can add operational context to Maintenance Requests and Work Orders through photographs, comments, and notes.

## Functional Scope

The capability associates contributions with the relevant request or order and makes them available during its operational lifecycle.

## Boundaries and Rules

Contributions must remain attached to the correct maintenance record and attributable in context. Attachment limits, formats, and retention are not defined by this Feature.
